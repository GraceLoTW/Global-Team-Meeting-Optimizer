Global Team Meeting Optimizer

Select a week and meeting duration, then analyze synthetic Outlook availability to find the fairest feasible time across global locations.

Demo only. All names, commitments, and availability data are synthetic.

Read me

Purpose

This prototype demonstrates how AI-assisted decision support can combine time-zone fairness, daylight-saving changes, awake-hour guardrails, and Outlook free/busy data to recommend meeting times for a globally distributed team.

How to use

  1. Select the week to analyze.
  2. Select a 30- or 60-minute meeting duration.
  3. Select Analyze Outlook availability.
  4. Review the top three options, weighted-score calculation, local times, daylight-saving status, and calendar conflicts.
  5. The manager retains the final scheduling decision.

Known limits

  • This is a browser-based demonstration using synthetic employees and synthetic Outlook commitments.
  • It does not connect to live Outlook calendars or send meeting invitations.
  • The awake window is fixed at 5:00 AM–11:00 PM for every participant.
  • The scoring weights are fixed at 60% time-zone comfort and 40% Outlook availability.
  • All attendees are treated as required and equally weighted.
  • It evaluates weekdays in 30-minute increments for only the listed demonstration weeks.
  • Holiday calendars, travel, personal preferences, and recurring-meeting history are not included.

4. Score transparency

The score is a weighted average, not an unexplained AI judgment.

Weighted score = (Time-zone comfort × 60%) + (Outlook availability × 40%)
  • Time-zone comfort: each attendee receives 100 during 8 AM–6 PM; the score declines toward 0 at the awake-window edges of 5 AM and 11 PM. The option uses the average across attendees.
  • Outlook availability: 100 when all required attendees are free; otherwise, the percentage of required attendees with no overlapping commitment.
  • Guardrail: times outside any attendee's 5 AM–11 PM window are excluded before scoring.
